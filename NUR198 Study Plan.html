🚫 Anti-wheel-spinning rules

  • Questions before notes. Always. Recall beats re-reading.
  • Never rewatch a whole video. Only the clip for the one thing you missed.
  • Stuck >5 min? Flag it, move on. Come back tomorrow — don't grind.
  • Teach it out loud. Can't explain it simply = that's your gap. Found it.
  • Track your misses and re-test them 2 days later. That gap is the whole game.
  • Done > perfect. Check the box, move on. Consistency wins this.
